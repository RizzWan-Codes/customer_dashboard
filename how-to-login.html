How to Login

Follow these steps to log in and use the Delivery App:

  1. Sign Up with your full name, phone number, email, and password.
  2. Check your email inbox (and spam folder) for a verification link.
  3. Click the link in the email to verify your account.
  4. After verification, go back to the Login page and enter your email + password.
  5. Once logged in, you’ll be redirected to your Customer Dashboard.
